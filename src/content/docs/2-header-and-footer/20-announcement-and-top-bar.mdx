## Announcement bar

Use the announcement bar for one to three short messages — a shipping threshold, free gift wrap, trade-in for store credit. Add one **Message** block per message.

- **All messages in one row** shows them side by side on desktop.
- **Rotate messages** shows one at a time. Turn on **Show arrows and counter** to let shoppers step through them, and set how long each one shows with **Seconds per message**.

Keep each message under about 60 characters so it fits on one line on phones.

Add a **Side note** to show short text at the left of the bar, such as *Free gift wrap on every order*, and a **Utility menu** to show links at the right, such as stockists and help. The utility menu replaces the counter. Both are hidden on phones.

## Top bar

The top bar is a slim strip above the header with social media icons, a short message, a utility menu and the currency and language selectors. Add it from the **Header** group with **Add section**. On phones only the message stays.

Choose a **Utility menu** to show links at the right of the bar, before the selectors. Social media links come from **Theme settings > Social media**.
