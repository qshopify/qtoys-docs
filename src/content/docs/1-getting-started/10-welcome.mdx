QToys is a Shopify theme for toy stores. Every toy sits on one **grow line** of six age bands, from newborn to eight, so parents can see at a glance what fits their child now and what comes next.

## What you get

- **The grow line everywhere** — on product cards, the product page, quick view, product rows, predictive search, the cart, collection banners and age filters, drawn from one product metafield.
- **Age tools** — "How old is your child?" age chips on the product page that say whether a toy is early, a good fit, at its peak or probably outgrown, an age check in the cart, and **My kids** in the customer account.
- **Skill tags** — Move, Think, Feel, Pretend and Create as colored dots and chips, with colors you choose.
- **Toy sections** — toy hero, age shop, skill tiles, gift finder, trade-in, play stages and box contents, alongside heroes, storytelling and commerce sections such as a bundle builder and shoppable image.
- **A complete store out of the box** — home page, collection, gift guide, product, Play Box, cart, search, blog, article, about, contact, FAQ, trade-in, wishlist and account pages, all built from sections you can rearrange.
- **Built-in Shopify features** — filtering, predictive search, pickup availability, subscriptions, gift cards, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay Installments, Follow on Shop and the customer account component.
- **Accessible by default** — keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip link, and age ranges always written out as text, never shown by color alone.
