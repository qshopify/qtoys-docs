Theme settings apply to every page. Open them from the theme editor by clicking the **paintbrush icon** in the left sidebar.

## Presets

QToys comes in five presets. Each one is a full set of theme settings plus its own home page, header and footer. Choose a preset when you add the theme from the Theme Store; after that, every setting below can be changed.

Every preset uses the **Shop by age** footer layout and the same product and collection templates. Page width, product cards and cart settings are the same in each preset.

## Logo and favicon

Your logo is set in the **Header** section (and, optionally, the **Footer** section), so you can size it per layout. Without a logo image, both show a text wordmark — which can be split into two colors with a small tag after it, like *my|oi toys*. The favicon — the small icon in the browser tab — is set here.

## Colors

QToys uses **color schemes**: named sets of background, text, border, button and accent colors. Every section has a **Color scheme** setting, so you can alternate light and tinted bands down a page while keeping every color pairing accessible.

Each scheme also has three grow line colors: **Age bands it fits** colors the bands a toy covers, **Next band** tints the band a child grows into next, and **Empty band** colors the rest of the line. Because they change per scheme, a grow line on a tinted section can use different colors from one on white.

<SettingsTable global="Colors" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep text readable">
When you change a scheme, check the contrast between **Background** and **Text**, and between **Button** and **Button label**. Aim for a ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text. Keep **Age bands it fits** clearly different from **Empty band** so the grow line stays easy to read.
</Callout>

## Typography

Choose two fonts from Shopify's font library: one for headings and one for body text. Rounded faces suit QToys. Bold and italic styles load automatically.

The defaults are **Fredoka** (bold) for headings and **Montserrat** for body text. The other presets use their own fonts — see [Presets](#presets). Use **Heading size** under **Shapes and sizes** to scale every heading at once.

## Product cards

These settings apply to every product card in the store — collection grids, featured collections, recommendations and search results. Cards also read a toy's age bands, skills, tile color, condition and rating when they're set; tag a product `badge:Bestseller` to show a badge. Whether cards show the grow line and skills is set under [Grow line](#grow-line).

Turn on **Show wishlist heart** to let shoppers save toys. Saved toys are kept in the shopper's browser; show them on a page that uses the *Wishlist* template, and choose that page in the header's **Wishlist page** setting for a heart with a count.

## Shapes and sizes

Shapes and sizes change the feel of every page at once:

- **Button shape** — **Square**, **Soft corners** or **Pill**.
- **Button text** — **Sentence case** or **Uppercase**.
- **Heading size** — scales every heading from 80% to 120% of the default.

## Grow line

The grow line reads each product's `custom.age_bands` metafield — a list whose values match your band names — and highlights the bands the toy fits. Products without it show no line. For how shoppers use it, see [How the grow line works](/docs/grow-line/how-the-grow-line-works).

### Age bands

- **Age bands** — six short labels, comma separated, youngest first. The defaults are `0–6m, 6–12m, 1–2y, 2–3y, 3–5y, 5–8y`. Use exactly the same names as the choices of your `custom.age_bands` metafield.
- **Stage names** — a friendly name per band, shown in menus and the footer, such as *Newborn* or *Big kid*.
- **Band edges in months** — one more number than bands, such as `0, 6, 12, 24, 36, 60, 96`. QToys uses them to place a child's age on the line.

### Age links

**All toys collection** is the collection that age links open — in menus, the footer, the product page and filters — filtered to one band. **Age filter** is the Search & Discovery filter parameter for the age bands metafield; the default `filter.p.m.custom.age_bands` works once you add that filter in the Shopify Search & Discovery app.

### Skills

**Skill names** sets five skills, comma separated — by default *Move, Think, Feel, Pretend, Create*. Match the choices of your `custom.skills` metafield. **Skill 1** to **Skill 5** set each skill's color for dots, chips and skill tiles.

### Product cards

**Show the grow line and age range** adds a thin grow line and a short range such as *6m – 2y* to every card. **Show skills** adds skill dots.
