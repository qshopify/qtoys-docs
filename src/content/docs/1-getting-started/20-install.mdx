## Choose a preset

QToys comes in five presets. Each one is a full set of theme settings — colors, fonts, skill colors, button shape — plus its own home page, header and footer.

<img width="800" height="80" src="/images/docs/presets.png" alt="The five presets side by side: QToys (Myoi Toys) with pink pill buttons, Sprout (Little Grove) in sage and deep green, Rocket (Rocket & Co) in white, royal blue and yellow, Pebble (Pebble & Oak) in warm linen with a stacked header, and Pop (Pop Party) in cream, purple and lemon." />

| Preset | Made for | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **QToys** | A warm, playful all-round toy store (demo brand Myoi Toys) | Pink and sky blue on soft white, rounded Fredoka headings, pill buttons |
| **Sprout** | Story-led wooden and natural toys (Little Grove) | Deep green on off-white and sage, Quicksand headings, soft corners |
| **Rocket** | Bold, high-volume stores (Rocket & Co) | Yellow buttons, royal blue bands, heavy Poppins headings, slideshow and countdown |
| **Pebble** | Calm Montessori stores (Pebble & Oak) | Warm brown on linen, muted skill colors, Outfit headings, soft corners |
| **Pop** | Bright party toys and gifts (Pop Party) | Purple and lemon on cream, Grandstander headings, gift finder on the home page |

Pick the preset closest to your catalog — every setting can be changed afterward. See [Presets](/docs/getting-started/theme-settings#presets) for the full comparison.

## Add the theme to your store

1. Open the QToys listing in the Shopify Theme Store and choose a preset from the style picker.
2. Click **Try theme** to preview it with your own products, or **Add theme** after purchase.
3. QToys appears in your **Theme library** in **Online Store > Themes**. It is not live until you publish it.

To try a second preset, add QToys again from the Theme Store with the other style selected. Each copy has its own settings, so you can compare them side by side in the theme library.

## Customize before you publish

Click **Customize** next to QToys in your theme library. The theme editor opens with your real products, collections and menus, so you can see exactly how your store will look.

A good first pass:

1. **Logo and colors** — set your logo or wordmark in the **Header** section, then open **Theme settings** (the paintbrush icon) for the favicon, color schemes and fonts. See [Theme settings](/docs/getting-started/theme-settings).
2. **Age data** — add the `custom.age_bands` metafield to your products, and `custom.skills` if you use skill tags. Products without age bands show no grow line. See [How the grow line works](/docs/grow-line/how-the-grow-line-works).
3. **Grow line** — in **Theme settings > Grow line**, choose your **All toys collection** so age links in menus, the footer and the product page have somewhere to go.
4. **Navigation** — the header uses your **Main menu** and the footer uses the menus you add as **Footer menu** blocks, from **Online Store > Navigation**.
5. **Home page** — replace the sample sections' images and copy, and remove any section you don't need.
6. **Product page** — open a product in the editor and arrange the product information blocks. See [Product page](/docs/products/product-page).

## Publish

When you're happy, go back to **Online Store > Themes**, click **⋯** next to QToys and choose **Publish**. Your previous theme stays in the library, so you can switch back at any time.

<Callout type="warning" title="Duplicate before you edit code">
If you or a developer plan to edit theme code, always duplicate the theme first (**⋯ > Duplicate**) and make changes on the copy. See [Custom code](/docs/advanced/custom-code).
</Callout>

## Install from a file

If you received QToys as a `.zip` file, go to **Online Store > Themes**, click **Add theme > Upload zip file**, and choose the file. Don't unzip it first.
