These sections put the [grow line](/docs/grow-line/how-the-grow-line-works) and the things toy shoppers ask about at the center of a page. Add them from **Add section** in the theme editor. Toys shown by age need a `custom.age_bands` metafield. See [Grow data](/docs/grow-line/grow-data).

## Toy hero

A home page opener: an eyebrow pill, a large heading, a line of text and two buttons on one side, and your image on a circle or blob on the other, with decorative sparkles, hearts or clouds around it. Wrap one word of the **Heading** in italics to underline it with a squiggle.

Pick a toy under **Best-for card** to float a small card on the image with that toy's grow line. Add **Trust point** blocks for short lines such as *Free shipping over $50*, each with its own dot color.

## Shop by age

The **Age shop** section turns the six age bands into buttons. Tapping one changes the toys below to those that fit that band, without reloading the page, and the **See all** link follows the chosen band.

- **Collection** — the toys to choose from. **Toys per age** sets how many show for each band.
- **Starting age band** — the band pressed when the page opens, from 1 (the first band) to 6.
- **Start at the shopper's saved child** — when the shopper has saved a child on their account page, the section opens at that child's band instead.

Add a **Band note** block to show a short line for one band, such as *Stack, push, pull* for *1–2y*. The **Band** must match a label in **Theme settings > Grow line**.

## Skill tiles

One tile per skill, in a tint of the skill's color, with an icon, the name, a line of text and the number of toys in its collection. Add a **Skill** block for each tile, choose its **Icon** and **Colour**, and pick a **Collection**. With no collection, the tile links to **Link** instead and shows no count.

## Gift finder

Three quick questions for shoppers buying for someone else's child: their age, a budget and an occasion. Each answer is a chip, and the button opens the **Results collection** with those filters applied. A summary line, such as *Birthday · 1–2y · $25 – $50*, follows the answers.

- The age chips are the six age bands. **Age chip selected at start** sets which one is pressed.
- Add a **Budget** block for each price range, with **From** and **Up to** in your store currency.
- Add an **Occasion** block for each occasion. Its **Filter value** is matched by **Occasion filter parameter**, a product tag by default.

<Callout type="note">
The gift finder builds a filtered collection link, so the filters must exist in the Search & Discovery app: the age bands metafield, price, and tags (or the metafield you use for occasions). See [Grow data](/docs/grow-line/grow-data#collection-filters).
</Callout>

## Trade-in

Explains your pre-loved program. Choose a **Layout**:

- **How it works: steps and grades** — numbered step cards (**Step** blocks) and the condition grades (**Grade** blocks), such as *Like new*, *Good* and *Well loved*.
- **Credit estimator** — shoppers pick the kind of toy (**Toy kind** blocks), what it cost new (**Price options**) and its condition (**Grade** blocks). The result card shows the estimated credit, worked out from the price, the grade's **Credit rate** and, when ticked, **Extra credit for toys bought here**.

<Callout type="warning">
The estimator only estimates store credit. It doesn't collect toys, create trade-ins or issue credit. You handle real trade-ins yourself, for example by pointing **Button link** at your contact page so shoppers can send you the details.
</Callout>

## Play stages

For product pages. Shows how one toy is played with at each age, as a row of cards, each with an age, a title and a line of text. The cards come from the product's `custom.play_stages` metafield, and the section hides on products without it. See [Play stages metafield](/docs/grow-line/grow-data#play-stages).

## Box contents

For a toy box sold in stages, such as a subscription box with one variant per age. Add a **Stage** block for each stage. Its **Stage** must match a value of the product option named in **Stage option name** (*Stage* by default) exactly, such as *6–12m*. The section follows the variant the shopper picks. Choose a **Layout**:

- **What's inside the selected stage** — the toys in the selected stage's box, up to four (**Toy 1** to **Toy 4**), with their skill and subtitle.
- **How the box grows (every stage)** — every stage in a row, with an icon and one line each, and the selected one marked *Starting here*.
