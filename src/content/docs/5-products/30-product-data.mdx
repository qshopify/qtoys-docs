QToys keeps product copy out of the theme. It reads optional product fields so that the same information shows consistently on cards, the product page, quick view and the cart. Add them in **Settings > Custom data > Products** as metafield definitions, in the `custom` namespace, then fill them in on each product. Anything you leave empty simply hides.

The grow line has its own setup guide. See [Grow data](/docs/grow-line/grow-data).

## Product metafields

| Metafield | Type | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `custom.age_bands` | List of single line text, with choices | The grow line and age range everywhere: cards, the product page, quick view, the cart, search and filters. Required for the grow line. The choices must match **Age bands** in **Theme settings > Grow line** exactly. |
| `custom.skills` | List of single line text, with choices | Skill dots on cards and skill chips in the product page's **Skills** block. The choices must match **Skill names** in **Theme settings > Grow line**. |
| `custom.tile_color` | Color | The background behind the product on cards, in the product gallery and in quick view — for example a soft pink behind a cut-out photo |
| `custom.subtitle` | Single line text | A short line under the product title on cards, in recommendation rows and in **Box contents**, for example *Five rings and a top bead*. On the product page, connect the **Title** block's **Subheading** to it. |
| `custom.play_stages` | JSON | The **Play stages** section on the product page. See [Grow data](/docs/grow-line/grow-data). |
| `custom.safety_standard` | Single line text | The safety panel of the **Safety & materials** block, for example *EN 71 · ASTM F963* |
| `custom.small_parts` | True or false | The safety panel: *No small parts* when false |
| `custom.test_report` | File | The safety panel: a link to download the test report |
| `custom.materials` | List of single line text | The materials panel |
| `custom.in_the_box` | List of single line text | The in the box panel |
| `custom.care` | Rich text | A care accordion in the **Safety & materials** block |
| `custom.condition` | Single line text, with choices | A pre-loved badge, such as *Like new*, *Good* or *Well loved*, on cards and in the cart |
| `custom.contents` | List of products | What's inside a set or bundle: the **What's inside** block on the product page lists these products |
| `reviews.rating` | Rating | Stars on cards, in quick view and in the product page's **Rating** block. Review apps fill this automatically. |

<Callout type="tip" title="Connect a metafield to a block">
Many block settings, such as the **Collapsible tab** block's **Content** or the **Pairs with** block's **Product**, can read a metafield instead of fixed text. In the theme editor, select the setting's **Connect dynamic source** icon and pick the metafield. Each product then shows its own value, and the block hides on products where it's empty.
</Callout>

## Collection and article metafields

| Owner | Metafield | Type | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collection | `custom.color` | Color | The collection's color in the **Collection banner**, collection tiles, filter chips and the all collections page |
| Article | `custom.product` | Product, or List of products | The toys the article is about: the *In this article* list on the article page, and the product in the blog's **Rows** layout |

Create collection metafields in **Settings > Custom data > Collections** and article metafields in **Settings > Custom data > Blog posts**.

## Filters

To let shoppers filter by age and skill, add product metafield filters on `custom.age_bands` and `custom.skills` in the **Search & Discovery** app, and optionally on `custom.condition`. QToys draws the age filter as the six grow line segments and the skills filter as colored chips. See [Filters](/docs/collections-and-search/collection-pages#age-and-skill-filters).

## Badges

Add a product tag that starts with `badge:` to show a badge on its card — for example `badge:Bestseller` or `badge:New`. Sale and sold-out badges appear automatically when a product has a compare-at price or is out of stock.

## Color swatches

Swatches come from Shopify's own color swatches. In the product's options, link a color option to the **Color** category metafield, or set a swatch color or image for each value. The variant picker shows them on the product page and in quick view.
