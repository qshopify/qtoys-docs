The product page is built from the **Product information** section. Everything in the buy box — title, price, grow line, variant picker, buy buttons, description — is a separate block, so you can reorder, remove or repeat them.

## Gallery

All product media shows in the gallery: images, Shopify-hosted videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos and 3D models (with *View in your space* on supported phones). When a shopper picks a variant that has its own image, the gallery jumps to it. On phones, shoppers swipe through the media. When the product has a `custom.tile_color` metafield, its media sit on that color — handy for cut-out or illustrated toy photos.

Choose a **Desktop gallery** layout:

- **Thumbnails on the side** — a column of thumbnails to the left of the main image. The default product template uses it.
- **Thumbnails below** — a row of thumbnails under the main image.
- **Stacked grid** — every image at once, in a two-column grid. The buy box stays in view while shoppers scroll.
- **Lead image, three below** — one large image, then the rest in a row of three. The **play-box** and **showcase** templates use it.

Choose a **Media shape** for the frame, and **Media fit**: **Fill** crops images to the frame, **Fit** shows them whole. Images of different shapes are fitted into the same frame, so a mixed set never breaks the layout.

<img width="642" height="553" src="/images/docs/product-gallery.png" alt="The gallery with thumbnails on the side: a column of thumbnails beside the main image of stacking rings on a pink tile." />

Turn on **Sticky add-to-cart bar** to keep the product title, price and buy button in reach while shoppers scroll.

## Grow line

The **Grow line** block shows which of the six age bands the toy suits, read from the product's `custom.age_bands` metafield, with the age range as a heading — for example *Best for 6 months – 2 years*. Turn off **Show "Best for…" heading** to show the line alone. See [How the grow line works](/docs/grow-line/how-the-grow-line-works).

With **Ask the child's age** on, age chips under the line ask *How old is your child?*. When a shopper picks an age — or has saved a child under **My kids** on the account page — that band is marked on the line and a message says how the toy fits: a little early, a good fit, right in the sweet spot, or probably outgrown. The age is kept in the shopper's browser only.

## Skills, safety and materials

- **Skills** shows the product's `custom.skills` as chips — for example *Move* and *Think* — tinted with the skill colors from **Theme settings > Grow line**.
- **Safety & materials** shows up to three panels from product metafields: the safety standard, whether there are small parts and a link to the test report; what the toy is made of; and what's in the box. Care instructions from `custom.care` follow in an accordion. Choose a **Layout** of **Stacked** or **Cards**. Panels without data are skipped, and the block hides when all are empty.

## Gift wrap, wishlist and trade-in

- **Gift wrap** adds a switch under the buy button. When it's on, the item is added to the cart with a *Gift wrap* line property, which you see on the order. The theme doesn't charge for it — if you want to charge for wrapping, sell it as its own product. Turn on **On by default** to start with the switch on.
- **Wishlist button** saves the product to the shopper's wishlist. The list is kept in the shopper's browser only: it isn't tied to a customer account, doesn't sync between devices and isn't visible to you in the admin. Saved toys show on a page using the **wishlist** template.
- **Trade-in note** shows a short line, such as *Outgrown it later? Trade it in for store credit.*, with a link to your trade-in page.

## Other buy box blocks

- **Title** and **Price** — the title can show a **Subheading**; connect it to `custom.subtitle` for per-product copy. The price shows compare-at prices, unit prices and a *tax included* note when your store shows prices with tax.
- **Text** — a line of text in **Eyebrow** or body style, for example *Pre-order* above the title.
- **Variant picker** — **Pills** or a **Dropdown**. Colors show as swatches from your product's color swatches. Sold-out combinations stay visible but are marked.
- **Buy buttons** — quantity, add to cart, accelerated checkout buttons and Shop Pay Installments messaging. Use **Button label override** and **Note under the button** for pre-orders.
- **Inventory status** — shows a low-stock message when the real inventory is at or under your **Low stock threshold**.
- **Pickup availability** — shows whether the selected variant can be picked up at your locations, with a link to all stores. Requires local pickup in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**.
- **Delivery estimate** — an estimated delivery window from your own cut-off time and business days.
- **Delivery and perks** — up to four short lines, such as free returns, as tiles, a stacked list or one row.
- **Gift card recipient** — lets shoppers send a gift card to someone else by email, with a message and send date. Shown on gift card products only.
- **Rating** — the product's `reviews.rating`, filled by review apps.
- **Pairs with** — one related product, as a link or as an add-on that's added to the cart with this one.
- **What's inside** — the products in a set, from the product's `custom.contents` metafield or the products you pick in the block.
- **Collapsible tab**, **Trust badges**, **Share**, **Breadcrumbs**, **Vendor** and **SKU** — supporting details. Each one hides itself when it has nothing to show.
- **Custom liquid** and **app blocks** — add review stars or other app features exactly where you want them.
