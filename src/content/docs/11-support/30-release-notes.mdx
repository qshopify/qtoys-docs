## 1.0.0

The first release of QToys — a theme for toy stores. Every toy sits on one grow line of six age bands, so parents see what fits now and what comes next.

### The grow line

- Six age bands, renameable and with their own month edges, drawn from the `custom.age_bands` metafield on product cards, the product page, quick view, product rows, predictive search, the cart drawer and cart page, collection banners and filters.
- "How old is your child?" on the product page: age chips, or a saved child, mark the band and tell the shopper whether the toy is early, a good fit, at its peak age or probably outgrown.
- Age check in the cart for saved kids.
- **My kids** in the customer account: names and birth months kept in the browser, each with its own grow line and a *Toys for …* link.
- Skills — Move, Think, Feel, Pretend and Create — as colored dots and chips, with colors in Theme settings.
- Search & Discovery filters on age bands are drawn as grow line segments, and skills as colored chips.

### New sections

- **Toy hero**, **Age shop** (a home page age picker), **Skill tiles** and **Gift finder**.
- **Trade-in**, with steps and a store credit estimator.
- **Play stages** — "One toy, four ways to play" on the product page.
- **Box contents** — what's inside each stage of a play box, and a timeline.

### Shopping

- Product page built from individual blocks, including the grow line, skills, safety, what's in the box, gift wrap, trade-in note, wishlist, variant picker, buy buttons, pickup availability, delivery, trust badges, collapsible tabs, share, custom liquid and app blocks.
- Accelerated checkout and Shop Pay Installments. Subscriptions, pre-orders and gift cards with recipient forms.
- Pre-loved toys with a condition badge on cards, the product page and the cart.
- Wishlist heart on cards and the product page, a count in the header, and a wishlist page.
- Quick view and quick add from product cards.
- Cart drawer, cart notification or cart page, with order notes, discounts and a free shipping goal.
- Filtering, sorting, pagination and predictive search. Related and complementary recommendations, and recently viewed products.

### Templates

- Product · play-box (stage variants with selling plans), Collection · gift-guide, Page · about, Page · trade-in and Page · wishlist, plus every standard template restyled for toys.

### Store-wide

- Five presets — QToys, Sprout, Rocket, Pebble and Pop — each with its own home page, header and footer.
- Four header layouts, mega menu or dropdown navigation with shop by age, a two-color wordmark, and sticky, compact and transparent options. Announcement bar and top bar.
- Four footer layouts, including Shop by age: the six age bands as large links along a line. Signup, footer menus, the toy rule, social media icons, Follow on Shop, payment icons, and country and language selectors.
- Shopify's customer account component in the header on desktop and mobile.
- Newsletter popup, cookie banner (with Shopify's Customer Privacy API) and info drawer.
- Five color schemes with grow line colors, and font pickers for headings and body text.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip link and WCAG AA contrast.
- [JavaScript events](/docs/advanced/developer-events) for the cart, variant selection, drawers, search, newsletter signups, saved kids and the wishlist.
