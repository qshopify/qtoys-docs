## How to reach us

Use the [support form](/support). Include your store URL, what you expected to happen and what happened instead, and a screenshot if you can. You'll get an automatic confirmation by email straight away.

## Response time

We reply to every request within **two business days** (Monday to Friday, Vietnam time, excluding public holidays). Critical bugs that stop customers from buying are fixed as a priority.

## Included in your purchase

- Help setting up and using QToys's features and settings, including the grow line and its metafields.
- Fixes for bugs in the theme's own code, delivered as theme updates.
- Guidance on which section or setting achieves what you want.

## Not included

- Custom code, design changes beyond the theme's settings, or work on a theme someone has edited.
- Installing, configuring or fixing third-party apps.
- Store setup outside the theme — products, shipping, payments, domains, and entering your products' age bands, skills and safety details.

For anything not included, we recommend hiring a [Shopify Partner](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory).
